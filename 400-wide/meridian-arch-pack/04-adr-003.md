# ADR-003: Use Auth0 as the External Identity Provider, Not an In-House Identity Service

## Context

Meridian Phase 1 requires a unified identity layer that works across web, mobile, and in-store POS. Tomás Reyes flagged a risk: building even a "thin" in-house identity service tends to accumulate auth complexity over time ("becoming a new monolith" — his phrase). The regulatory context makes this worse: GDPR right-to-erasure, CCPA data subject rights, and PSD2 SCA on EU checkout all require auditable auth flows, token lifecycle management, and MFA — features that take 6–12 months to build correctly and are outside the junior MRG team's core competency.

## Decision

We will use Auth0 as the external identity provider for all Meridian surfaces (web, mobile, POS). The Meridian Identity Service is a thin proxy: it validates JWTs issued by Auth0 and maintains a local Redis session cache for sub-100ms token checks on the POS hot path. Auth0 handles user provisioning, MFA, OIDC/OAuth2 flows, and PSD2 SCA compliant step-up auth. No passwords or identity credentials are stored in Meridian-owned databases.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Build an in-house identity service from scratch** — rejected because implementing OIDC, MFA, PSD2 SCA step-up auth, GDPR right-to-erasure, and CCPA data subject rights correctly would consume 4–6 months of engineering on a junior team that needs to deliver cart and checkout in the same window. Tomás Reyes explicitly flagged this as a "new monolith" risk — the maintenance surface grows with every auth feature added.

2. **Use Keycloak (open-source, self-hosted)** — rejected because Keycloak requires dedicated ops expertise and infrastructure management (JVM tuning, HA clustering, upgrade cycles). The $42M programme budget and junior team cannot absorb a self-hosted IAM platform in Phase 1; the operational cost is comparable to building in-house without the Auth0 SLA.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Auth0 Enterprise SLA (99.99% uptime) offloads auth reliability risk. PSD2 SCA step-up auth and MFA are Auth0-managed features — Meridian gets compliant flows without implementing them. GDPR/CCPA subject-access and erasure are handled at the Auth0 tenant level.
- **Negative:** Auth0 is a critical external dependency — an Auth0 incident affects every Meridian surface simultaneously. Mitigation: the Identity Service's Redis session cache allows continued token validation for up to 15 minutes without an Auth0 round-trip (covered by NFR #6, K 4.W.7). Auth0 Enterprise licensing adds approximately $80K–$120K/yr to the programme operating cost.

## Agent-Readable Summary

Auth0 is the sole identity provider for all Meridian authentication flows. Do not introduce a second identity store, a custom JWT signing key, or a local user-credential database in any Meridian service. The Identity Service is a thin JWT-validation proxy with a Redis cache; it MUST NOT implement auth business logic (registration, password reset, MFA) — those belong to Auth0. Any new service that needs to verify user identity MUST call the Identity Service, not Auth0 directly, to benefit from the session cache.

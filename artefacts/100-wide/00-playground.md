# Narrow Industry Playground — Meridian Italy Click & Collect Segment

**Playground (1 line)**
Italy fashion & lifestyle omnichannel retailer, ~€280–350M annual revenue, 180 stores concentrated in urban centers (Milan, Rome, Naples, Turin, Florence). Regulatory pressure: GDPR, PSD2 SCA, local payment methods (Postepay, Satispay), Italian VAT e-commerce rules.

**Dominant Business Problem (1 line)**
Click & collect cancellations at pickup (7% of orders): customers order online for in-store pickup, inventory shown as available on SAP ERP doesn't sync to checkout platform, staff can't locate items at pickup, customers cancel and buy from competitors; direct revenue loss + brand frustration in highest-margin urban segment.

**Three Economics of the Segment**
- Italy is Meridian's second-largest EU market by urban density and click & collect adoption (43% of online orders routed to in-store pickup vs. 31% EU average); 7% cancellation rate = €19–25M annual revenue leak in Italy alone.
- Competitors (Zalando, About You) invest heavily in real-time inventory visibility for click & collect as a retention lever; Meridian's SAP-to-platform sync gap is creating a churn vector in the most profitable (urban, repeat, high-frequency) customer segment.
- Real-time inventory sync from SAP to headless platform is achievable within Phase 1 timeline (Q3 2026); Italy is the pilot segment for this operational lift, with fastest ROI payback (6–9 months from launch).

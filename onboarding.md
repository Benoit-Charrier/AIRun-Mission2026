## Running Case

**Reference Case A — Meridian Retail**

**One-paragraph scope:** Meridian Retail is building a unified omnichannel commerce platform covering identity, cart, and loyalty across 1,400 physical stores and their digital channels. The value hypothesis is that unifying the customer experience across channels will increase loyalty conversion and support agent efficiency. The user-facing surface is a web and mobile storefront plus an internal support dashboard. There is an existing codebase across multiple services. Customer transaction and loyalty data spans several years. The team includes engineers, QA, designers, and a delivery manager.

**Gut-check:**

- Why build it? Unified identity and loyalty across 1,400 stores drives higher conversion and reduces support cost.
- Who uses it, and where? Customers on web/mobile; support agents in an internal dashboard.
- Is there code? Yes — a multi-service platform codebase.
- Is there data? Yes — years of transaction, cart, and loyalty data.
- Is there a team? Yes — cross-functional: engineering, QA, design, and delivery.
- Anything missing? No ADRs or agent specs yet — those are produced in later modules.

# Slide 1 - Context
MRG is consolidating 22 regional stacks into one commerce platform. Identity and loyalty fragmentation currently block consistent customer journeys, campaign performance, and reliable cross-channel execution during migration.

Speaker note: We are solving the core data-fragmentation bottleneck that limits platform value.

# Slide 2 - Problem
Customers hold multiple IDs and loyalty accounts across regions. Outcomes: split balances, weak targeting, support escalations, and slower migration decisions because teams cannot trust a single customer view.

Speaker note: Fragmentation creates both revenue leakage and execution drag.

# Slide 3 - Opportunity
An identity-resolution copilot can match, score, and reconcile duplicate profiles at migration speed, enabling one customer identity and loyalty continuity without waiting for full legacy retirement.

Speaker note: This is a practical enabler for Phase 1, not a later luxury.

# Slide 4 - Solution
Use confidence-based matching: auto-merge high-confidence profiles, route medium-confidence to operations review, hold low-confidence for adjudication, and maintain full audit trail plus rollback for contested merges.

Speaker note: The design prioritizes trust, reversibility, and controlled automation.

# Slide 5 - Value
Targets in two migration waves: reduce duplicate active-loyalty profiles by 40%, achieve 95% merge precision, and reduce identity-related support tickets by 25%.

Speaker note: These KPIs align with customer trust, retention, and operational throughput.

# Slide 6 - ROI
Base case: one-time cost EUR3.0M, annual run cost EUR1.7M, annual value EUR6.75M, annual net benefit EUR5.05M, payback 7.1 months.

Speaker note: Financial case is attractive, but pilot gate requires benchmark lock and +/-30% sensitivity guardrails; if precision drops below 90% or SLA fails, spend is rephased.

# Slide 7 - Risks
Main risks: bad merges, manual-review bottlenecks, and weak benchmark quality. Mitigations: strict confidence thresholds, 72-hour review SLA, evidence-based merge rules, and mandatory benchmark replacement for unverified assumptions.

Speaker note: Governance quality determines value realization; capacity model and gates are explicit: queue depth under one week and SLA attainment at or above 95% in pilot.

# Slide 8 - Ask
Approve pilot in Italy plus one EU market: funding for migration squad, identity operations reviewers, and governance support to run controlled waves with measurable outcomes.

Speaker note: We need resources to execute the pilot with operational discipline.

# Slide 9 - Timeline
Weeks 1-4: data profiling and merge-policy setup. Weeks 5-8: pilot matching and review workflow. Weeks 9-12: production rollout wave 1, KPI tracking, threshold tuning.

Speaker note: Twelve weeks gets us to a measurable production outcome.

# Slide 10 - Next Steps
Lock benchmark baselines, confirm ownership, launch pilot charter, and schedule steering checkpoint after wave 1 with go/no-go criteria for broader regional scale.

Speaker note: Scale path is phased (tier-1, tier-2, regional) with hard gates on duplicate reduction, SLA performance, and cost per merged identity before each expansion.

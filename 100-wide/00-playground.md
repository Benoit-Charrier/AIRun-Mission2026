1) Industry + sub-segment: Mid-market omnichannel retail (home goods, electronics, fashion) running cross-channel ecommerce + in-store pickup.
2) Region: Italy as the launch market, with interoperability requirements across EU country stacks.
3) Revenue band: EUR 1B-EUR 5B annual digital commerce value in-scope for the modernization wave.
4) Regulatory pressure: GDPR and PSD2 SCA.
5) Dominant business problem (measurable pain): Around 7% of click-and-collect orders are canceled at pickup due to inventory mismatch, driving conversion loss and trust erosion.

## Segment economics (3 bullets)

- Growth with margin pressure: Digital already contributes a large and growing share, but margin is pressured by promotions, returns, and fulfillment complexity in omnichannel retail.
- Fulfillment economics are inventory-accuracy-driven: The ~7% click-and-collect cancellation rate from phantom stock creates direct revenue leakage and avoidable operating cost.
- Identity and loyalty are profit levers: Fragmented accounts suppress repeat purchase and campaign efficiency, while unified identity/loyalty can lift retention and customer lifetime value.

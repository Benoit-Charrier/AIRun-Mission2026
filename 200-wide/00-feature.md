- **Product:** Meridian retail site and app (omnichannel, 1,400 stores, 22 countries)
- **Feature:** AI availability assistant on the product page — estimates whether an item is genuinely collectable at a nearby store before the shopper reserves it
- **Target user:** Click-&-collect shoppers who reserve online to avoid a wasted trip and currently cannot trust the stock indicator
- **Expected behaviour:** Given the shopper's location and a selected product, the assistant queries SAP inventory sync + store-level signals and returns a confidence-labelled availability verdict ("likely available", "uncertain — check with store", "not available") before the shopper completes the reservation
- **Success signal:** Phantom-stock cancellation rate at pickup drops from ~7% to ≤ 2%, measured weekly per region
- **Out of scope:** Reserve-and-hold functionality, real-time POS polling, and cross-store stock reallocation
